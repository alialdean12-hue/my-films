# Art direction / production prompts

Original illustrations (not archival or traced frames). English briefs below describe the commissioned cels; the seven lossless source plates in `assets/paint/` are the actual generated results. A consistent late-1980s/1990s science-anime style, tapered navy ink lines, flat planes of cel shading, and no letters, logos or photorealism. Palette remapped in code, rather than trusting an image model to obey exact hexes.

- **character** — “Original hand-inked 2D Japanese science anime character cel … 2013 Canadian astronaut Chris Hadfield as a stylized middle-aged anime man … neat dark moustache only … navy short-sleeve plain polo shirt … no patches … entire background perfectly uniform bright chroma green.” Three-quarter portrait and collar. Skin, iris highlights, cheek shadow, two-state eyelid and character registration in the animation.
- **observe** — Edit of the original unkeyed character painting, same model and green: “he is thoughtfully watching the experiment BELOW HIM … chin mildly tucked and gaze noticeably lowered toward the lower middle … no speech, no invented dialogue.” Used for the look down and lifted-gaze button.
- **wring** — “Extreme close-up foreground of two bare adult hands … left hand grasping the LEFT end, right hand grasping the RIGHT end of the SAME stretched horizontal twisted white cotton washcloth … transparent continuous rounded sheath of water … no cascade … no face … flat colors … no text.” The hands and twist were separated; the background was discarded. The experimental sleeve, sheen, moving meniscus, knitted hems and eight wringing poses are assembled/drawn in code.
- **cabin** — “A single vertical 9:16 interior background plate … lived-in pressurized ISS laboratory module … overhead harnesses and loops of restrained cables … one main practical warm cream lamp at upper right … no people … no large windows or sci-fi spacecraft/fantasy holograms.”
- **earth** — “Earth from low orbit … curving sunlit limb cuts a graceful diagonal … blue oceans, warm coastlines and beautiful ribboned white clouds … only the Earth and sky, no spacecraft.” The truss, arrays and orbital path are original SVG drawings, not a map of a specific ground track.
- **desk** — “Overhead shot of an ordinary Canadian school desk … open cream sketchbook … blank writing surfaces … two distinct sharpened pencils … a folded plain white facecloth … no printed or handwritten text.” An illustrative diagram and pencil acting track were added; no invented student quotation or private likeness.
- **sink** — “Domestic off-white porcelain wash basin viewed slightly from above … centered oval metal drain … faucet and two simple handles … air space in the middle above bowl free for animating a droplet falling … faucet OFF.” A didactic comparison, not purported footage of the experiment.

The backgrounds and model paintings are separable editable assets. The colour script, action drawings, timings, attention to safe areas and music were made specifically for this film. There is no generated replacement presented as a photograph.
